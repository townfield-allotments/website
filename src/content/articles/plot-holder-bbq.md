We held our first barbecue for plot holders on 18 October and although it was quite late in the season we were blessed with a mainly sunny day. Luckily the wind held off and with a few extra layers it was actually very pleasant!

Around 30 people attended, bringing their own drinks and food, including burgers and sausages to cook on our big barbecue. There was much friendly allotment chat over a glass or two of wine and even an informal chilli sauce and chutney tasting, as plot holders got to sample sauces made by fellow allotmenteers.

Folks participated in a fun quiz too, before packing up and heading home after a very enjoyable and relaxed afternoon. We plan to hold another next year, but preferably a little earlier to be sure of good weather!
