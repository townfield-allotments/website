Amy leads a small team who have a real passion for foraging and living from the land. They have recently held some events up at Townfield which included a walk around Hob Hey Woods before returning to the allotments to sample some of their finds.

As a thank you for hosting on the allotments, Amy ran two sessions exclusively for the tenants.

### 🥒 Day One: Pickling and Preserving

Amy is an absolute expert and her passion overflowed as we discussed all things pickling. Tenants brought a variety of vegetables from their plots. We were able to get really hands-on, chopping and creating our own concoctions of pickles, which was quite extreme. Amy brought a variety of spices to try, which added layers of complexity and the ability to change to taste.

We all got to try some of Amy's samples and if you ever get the chance, you must try her Japanese rose hip sauce. Unbelievable creation, but sadly the recipe remains her secret.

### 🍏 Day Two: Community Apple Press

The sun was shining and the event was very well attended, especially by many local children who all arrived bearing apples. The tenants were also able to provide many of the apples from their plots and soon we had a mountain of apples to chop ready for milling.

Graham led this event with his very traditional (very old!) grinder. The children filled the trough and the apples then went into the press. Graham soon had the production line organised, and the apple juice was flowing, with dozens of bottles filled on the day.

Amy also provided samples of apple creations with butter, jams, pickles, and caramel-covered delights. Their expertise on what can be created and the foraged flavours you can include is amazing. Everybody left with the confidence to explore different flavours and excited to play with new ingredients and great ideas.
