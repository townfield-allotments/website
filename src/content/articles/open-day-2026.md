On 18 July we held our annual Allotment Open Day, where we welcomed visitors to our fantastic site close to Hob Hey wood with extensive views across to the Weaver Valley and the Peak District beyond. We were lucky with the weather and visitors enjoyed exploring the allotment plots to see the wide range of vegetables, fruit and flowers produced and also had an opportunity to buy honey and candles made possible by the allotment bees!

![Children watering a raised bed](/images/openday26/027.jpeg) ![Children relaxing by a shed among the flowers](/images/openday26/051.jpeg) ![Visitors relaxing in deckchairs](/images/openday26/003.jpeg)

As usual there were welcoming cuppas, delicious homemade cakes and a barbecue on the go, and visitors mingled, enjoying upbeat music from local musicians. There was a scarecrow trail and lots of fun activities for the children too.

![Food being served at the barbecue](/images/openday26/059.jpeg) ![Visitors enjoying food](/images/openday26/047.jpeg) ![Families sitting on the grass](/images/openday26/013.jpeg)

![Children at the Scarecrow Trail sign](/images/openday26/000.jpeg) ![The Kid Zone](/images/openday26/062.jpeg) ![Face painting](/images/openday26/060.jpeg)

Our raffle was hugely popular again, with plot holders and some local businesses donating prizes.

The Allotment Open Day is a big event in our calendar and is a vital part of our fundraising which helps us to maintain the site. This includes keeping our lawnmowers, strimmers, etc in good working order, providing watering facilities and a portaloo for the benefit of everyone.

![Volunteers at the refreshments stall](/images/openday26/054.jpeg) ![A stall at the Open Day](/images/openday26/036.jpeg) ![Plot holders chatting at a stall](/images/openday26/049.jpeg)

> **Many thanks** to all those who donated raffle prizes and cakes and to those who set up and ran stalls, put gazebos up (and down) and prepared for our guests. We really wouldn't be able to hold the day at all without our willing allotment holders getting involved. Look out for next year's date - all are welcome!
