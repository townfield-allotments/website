---
title: Chilli Sauce
date: 2026-11-23T10:05:00Z
description: A hot, smooth chilli sauce made with ripe tomatoes and apple. Bottled in sterilised jars, it will keep for six months or more.
serves: Makes about 1 litre (3–4 small jars)
time: About 1½–2 hours
ingredients:
  - 200ml malt vinegar
  - 450g ripe tomatoes
  - 450g hot chillis
  - 1 large cooking apple
  - 15g salt
  - 1 medium onion
  - 85g sugar
  - 2 cloves garlic
---

1. Cut the tomatoes in half and cook in a stainless steel pan. When they are soft, remove the skins.
2. Chop the chillis, onion, apple and garlic and add to the pan. Cook until the onions are soft.
3. With a hand blender, blitz until smooth.
4. Add the vinegar and boil gently until the mixture is reduced and the consistency is that of a thick soup.
5. Add the sugar and salt. Bring back to the boil, stirring to make sure the sauce does not burn on the bottom of the pan.
6. Pour into sterilised jars and seal.

It will keep for six months or more.
