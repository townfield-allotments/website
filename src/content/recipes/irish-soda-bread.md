---
title: Will's Irish Soda Bread
date: 2026-11-23T10:00:00Z
description: Based on his Mum's recipe and one in Tim Allen's Ballymaloe Bread book. No yeast and no proving — just mix, shape and bake.
serves: Makes 1 loaf
time: About 45 minutes
ingredients:
  - 400g flour — a mix of strong white bread flour and wholemeal (I usually go for 150g white and 250g brown)
  - 50g porridge oats
  - 25g sesame seeds (optional)
  - 1 tsp brown sugar
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda, sieved
  - 350ml buttermilk (about one and a half cartons of supermarket buttermilk)
  - 1 egg
---

1. Turn the oven on to 220°C.
2. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
3. For the liquid, combine the buttermilk and beat in the egg.
4. Add the liquid to the bowl, working it in with a wooden spoon. The mix should be wet but not too sloppy. Add more liquid if it's too dry and more flour if it's too wet.
5. Dust a baking tray or Welsh planc with flour and push the mix onto the tray with the wooden spoon. The loaf should be oval or round.
6. Cut a cross in the centre of the loaf — to let the fairies out.
7. Put it directly in the middle of the hot oven and bake for 15 minutes. Then reduce the temperature to 180–190°C and bake for a further 20 minutes.

You can vary the proportion of white and wholemeal flour to suit your taste.
